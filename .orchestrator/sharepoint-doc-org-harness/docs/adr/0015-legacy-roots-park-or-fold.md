# Leftover VincePersonal roots park or fold

VincePersonal already has `00_Inbox`–`06_Reference` plus leftover roots (`artifacts`, `Command Center`, `cursor-inbox`, `PLX.io`, `Projects`, log dumps, loose files). Live work folds into `00`–`06`. Generated dumps park under `06_Reference/legacy-2026`. `cursor-inbox` drains into `00_Inbox` and stops being a save path. We do not keep a second taxonomy at the SharePoint root.
